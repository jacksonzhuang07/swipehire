Privacy Policy

Plain language, no legal fog. Last updated: November 2026.

What we store

To run the matching service we store: your account email and role; your resume and any supporting documents you upload (cover letters, references, project write-ups, transcripts, certifications) and the facts extracted from them into your Master Profile; your profile photo or employer logo; your preferences (target titles, locations, salary floor, remote-only); your swipes; the application packages we prepare for you, including tailored resumes and any answers built only from your stored facts; matches and the messages you exchange through them; and — only if you choose to add them — the board identifiers on your Connections page and a hashed pairing token for the browser extension.

What we never store

We never store your LinkedIn, Indeed, or any other job-board password. There is no password field for those services anywhere in SwipeHire. Linked-account records hold only the username/email you type and a status. Extension pairing tokens are stored hashed, the same way passwords are.

Email sign-in codes

If you sign in with an email code, we store only a hash of the code, its expiry, and an attempt counter — never the code itself after it is used. When no email service is configured, SwipeHire runs in a clearly-labelled demo mode where the code is shown on the sign-in screen instead of being emailed; the sign-in screen always tells you which mode you are in.

Job data

Job listings come from public job-board APIs and employer posts on SwipeHire. Scanning them does not share your profile with those boards. An application package stays inside SwipeHire until an executor you control (for example the browser extension, working in your own browser) reports that it was submitted.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your resume or documents with employers except the candidates' directory information employers already see inside the service (name, title, skills, summary, photo) and any tailored resume attached to an application you chose to prepare or submit.

Deleting your data

Profile → “Delete my account” permanently deletes your account and everything scoped to it: documents, Master Profile, swipes, application packages, connections, extension tokens, matches and messages. There is no undo.

Demo deployments

Demo copies of SwipeHire may run on temporary storage, meaning data can disappear on restart; the footer of a demo deployment says so. Do not rely on a demo deployment for anything you cannot afford to lose.
