Terms of Use

Plain language. Last updated: November 2026.

What SwipeHire does

SwipeHire compiles a Master Profile from documents you provide, ranks job listings for you, prepares application packages (including tailored resumes built only from your stored facts), and matches candidates with employers who mutually right-swipe. Application statuses are honest: an application is marked Submitted only when an executor reports a real submission — everything else is labelled Prepared, Queued, or Needs review.

What SwipeHire does not do

We do not guarantee interviews, offers, or employment. We never invent experience, skills, or answers on your behalf: screening answers come only from your stored facts, and unknowns are flagged for your review. We never ask for or store your passwords to LinkedIn, Indeed, or any other job board.

Your responsibilities

Everything you upload must be true and yours to share. If you use the browser extension or a linked-account session, you are responsible for complying with the terms of the job boards you apply through — the extension works in your own browser, under your own accounts. You are responsible for reviewing an application before it is submitted anywhere a final human click is required.

Employers

Employers may only contact matched candidates about genuine roles. Posting a job means the role exists as described.

Accounts

One account per person. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from your Profile. Demo accounts (labelled, password “demo”) exist for trying the service and may be removed at any time.

Changes

If these terms change materially, the updated date above changes with them. Continuing to use SwipeHire after a change means you accept the new terms.
